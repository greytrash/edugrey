# Prompts de generación (Kling 3.0 Omni, image_to_video)

Ambos spots parten de los fotogramas reales del prototipo como imágenes de
referencia para clavar el estilo litográfico. Parámetros comunes:
`duration=15`, `aspect_ratio=16:9`, `resolution=1080p`, `enable_audio=true`.

## Spot A — Dynamic

- `image_1`: `docs/preview.png`
- `prefer_multi_shots=true`

> Fast-paced high-energy cinematic spot built from image 1, preserving its
> exact visual style: a flat cel-shaded surrealist driving world finished as a
> high-contrast lithograph print - bold ink outlines, ordered-dither engraved
> shadows, paper grain, cream/black/rust-red palette, giant engraved red
> seal-sun in the sky. Multi-shot kinetic montage: (1) rear chase camera
> slammed low behind the black car as it rockets down the endless straight
> desert highway, double yellow lines strobing past; (2) hard cut - whip pan
> past a monolithic engraved tower and the impossible floating black diamond;
> (3) hard cut - the sky snaps to sunset, the huge red seal-sun looming closer
> over the horizon; (4) hard cut - night and rain, graphite rain streaks
> etching the frame, taillights glowing red, the car drifting across both
> lanes throwing spray; (5) final shot - the car races on under the giant red
> sun and the frame freeze-frames like a freshly pulled lithograph print.
> Aggressive kinetic camera, motion blur rendered as engraved streaks, punchy
> rhythmic editing. The entire video keeps the flat printed lithograph look -
> no photorealism. Audio: roaring engine, whoosh cut transitions, driving
> percussive electronic score, rain hiss, one final deep bass hit.

## Spot B — Educational (retro-futurista + psicodélico)

- `image_1`: `docs/preview.png` · `image_2`: `docs/preview-night-rain.png`
- `prefer_multi_shots=false`

> Calm educational explainer spot in the exact style of image 1 (day) and
> image 2 (night rain): a flat cel-shaded surreal driving world finished as a
> high-contrast lithograph print with bold ink outlines, ordered-dither
> engraved shadows and paper grain. Slow, steady cinematography: a smooth
> dolly glides forward above the straight desert highway while the black car
> cruises below between the double yellow lines. A calm, warm narrator
> voiceover says: Welcome to Dalinian Roads. Here, the road is real - the
> world is not. Ink outlines. Engraved shadows. Paper grain. A living
> lithograph, printed sixty times every second. As the narrator speaks,
> retro-futuristic schematic overlays fade in: thin cream blueprint lines,
> vintage 1970s science-documentary diagram arrows and small labels pointing
> at the giant red seal-sun, the floating black diamond and the road. The sky
> slowly turns psychedelic: the engraved paper clouds swirl into a gentle
> kaleidoscope, the giant red seal-sun pulses through rust-red, magenta and
> violet color cycles, the far mountains ripple like melting wax in Dali
> fashion - while the road below stays perfectly sharp, high-contrast and
> legible. In the final seconds the scene gently morphs from the day of
> image 1 into the night rain of image 2, graphite rain streaks etching in as
> the taillights glow. The whole video keeps the flat printed lithograph
> look - no photorealism. Audio: soft ambient retro-synthesizer arpeggios,
> faint vinyl crackle, gentle engine hum under the calm narration.
